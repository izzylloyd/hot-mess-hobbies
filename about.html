The Story

One LLC. Every hobby I refuse to quit.

Hot Mess Hobbies, LLC exists because I have never once stuck to a single hobby. I get into something, get a little too into it, and eventually someone asks "wait, can I buy one of those?" This is the answer.

Right now that means handmade tropical terrariums and football cards — two things that have nothing to do with each other, which is kind of the point. Instead of starting a new business every time I fall down a new rabbit hole, everything lives under one roof. As new obsessions turn into side hustles, they'll show up here too.

What This Is About

Actually Handmade

Every terrarium is built by hand, one at a time. No two are identical.

Genuinely a Hobby

These are things I'd be doing anyway. Buying from Hot Mess Hobbies supports the habit directly.

Always Expanding

This is built to grow — more hobbies, more little product lines, same one LLC.
